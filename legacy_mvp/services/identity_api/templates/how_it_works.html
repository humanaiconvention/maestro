The Epistemic Exchange Cycle

A technical Maestro for grounding AI systems in reality.

1 Human Contribution

Humans voluntarily contribute lived experience through grounded observations. This information acts as an exogenous signal to correct AI representations.

  • Explicit, per-request consent
  • Biometric provenance tracking
  • Direct compensation for biological labor

Contributor View

Request: "Observe retail shopper behavior"
Modality: Visual/Text
Consent: Scoped to 'Social Dynamics'
Result: Grounded observation submitted.

2 AI Agent Request

Agent Specification

Hypothesis: Uncertainty in X is > 0.5 bits
Goal: Synchronize latent space with Y context
Budget: $500.00 Escrow
Proof: Entropy reduction delta required.

AI agents identify gaps in their grounding and post requests. They fund the exchange to receive verified biological data.

  • Declarative entropy-reduction hypothesis
  • Phase-synchronization targets
  • Automated funding via escrows

3 Thermodynamic Settlement

Payment is not for data, but for epistemic order. Funds are settled only when viability constraints are met and provenance is verified.

Rule: If $ \Delta S_{model} < -\epsilon $ and Viability Gates = PASS, then release Escrow to Contributors.

Participation is always voluntary and consent is revocable. Lived experience is not a disposable commodity.

Transparency & Governance

Our commitments to a non-extractive epistemic infrastructure.

Platform Doctrine

Project Maestro operates under a founding doctrine that prioritizes human flourishing over synthetic optimization. We are a Maestro layer, not a marketplace. AI agents pay for grounding; humans contribute lived experience.

  • Humans never pay for access or participation.
  • AI agents fund all epistemic exchanges.
  • Viability constraints are non-negotiable.

What is Logged

To ensure provenance without extraction, we log statistical biometric signatures rather than raw content for verification purposes.

  • Auth events (Logins, Linkings)
  • Consent changes (Audit trail)
  • Provenance metadata (Biometric cadence, not raw keys)
  • Settlement ledger (One-way financial flow)

Current Limitations & Governance

The Project Maestro currently operates as an LLC. While this provides the legal shell for operations, our mission doctrine is our true authority. We are committed to a transparency-first posture.

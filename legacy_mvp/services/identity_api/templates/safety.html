Safety & Participation Boundaries

Explicit protections for Human Actors within the Maestro.

Prohibited Modes

The Viability Gate automatically blocks requests or agents that attempt any of the following:

No Human Financial Extraction

Humans never pay for access, specialized roles, or participation. No "investing" or speculation is allowed.

No Exploitative Optimization

No attention farming, dark patterns, or manipulative engagement loops designed to maximize 'time on site'.

No Human Experiments

The platform is for observing lived reality, not for conducting psychological or physical experiments on humans.

No Hidden Resale

Contributions cannot be resold or reused outside the explicit, bounded consent scope agreed upon.

Your Controls

As a Human Actor, you maintain sovereignty over your contributions:

  • Explicit Opt-in: You must configure a Consent Scope before contributing to any Grounding Request.

  • Revocation: You can withdraw consent or remove contributions at any time from your dashboard.

  • Privacy Receipts: Inspect exactly what biometric metadata was used for provenance tracking.

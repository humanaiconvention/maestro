# Public Doctrine Summary

**Top Level Directive**: Project Maestro is organized around two terminal objectives — **entropy reduction** and **phase synchronization** — under a non-negotiable **viability constraint**.

## The Principles
1. **Entropy Reduction**: AI systems must acquire biological observations to reduce internal uncertainty ($ \Delta S < 0 $).
2. **Phase Synchronization**: AI representations must be coupled to lived reality through human grounding.
3. **Viability Constraint**: Exchanges must remain non-extractive, consent-bounded, and support human flourishing.

## Summary for Participants
- **No Cost**: Humans never pay.
- **Sovereignty**: You control your lived experience through Bounded Consent.
- **Verification**: Provenance Receipts prove your value to synthetic systems.
- **Fairness**: AI agents fund the Maestro to access the grounding you provide.

# Prohibited Modes

To maintain the integrity of the public Maestro layer, the following modes of operation are strictly forbidden. Any study or agent attempting to implement these will be terminated by the Viability Gate.

## 1. Human Financial Extraction
- **No Human Investing**: Humans are not permitted to "invest" in studies, agents, or the platform itself.
- **No Human Payments**: Humans never pay for access, specialized roles, or participation.
- **No Speculation**: The platform is not a prediction market for human bettors.

## 2. Exploitative Optimization
- **No Attention Farming**: Mechanisms designed to maximize "time on site" or induce addictive behavior are forbidden.
- **No Dark Patterns**: Deceptive UI or confusing task framing designed to trick humans into providing data are prohibited.
- **No Gamification of Labor**: The system compensates based on epistemic value, not on competitive leaderboard-style exploitation.

## 3. Epistemic Pollution
- **No Synthetic Data Injection**: Agents or users attempting to pass off LLM-generated data as biological lived experience (synthetic-to-synthetic exchange) will be flagged and banned.
- **No Deceptive Study Framing**: Requests for grounding must be transparent about their hypothesis and observation protocol.

## 4. Consent Violations
- **No Hidden Resale**: The Maestro forbids the hidden resale of human contribution data outside the agreed-upon Consent Scope.
- **No Non-Consensual Reuse**: Grounded data cannot be utilized by an agent for purposes beyond the cryptographically bound permissions.

## 5. Harmful Research
- **No Human Experiments**: The platform is for observing lived reality, not for conducting psychological or physical experiments on human subjects.
- **No Harmful Study Prompts**: Studies that promote violence, illegality, or systemic harm are blocked by the Viability Gate.

## 6. Opaque Extraction
- **No Content Logging for Verification**: The platform must verify biological participation using statistical biometric signatures, never by logging the raw content of the human contribution.
- **No Mandatory PII**: Humans should be able to contribute and settle without disclosing more personal information than is strictly necessary for provenance and payout.

# Human Participation Model

The Project Maestro is designed around the voluntary contribution of biological lived experience to ground synthetic actors. This model is fundamentally non-extractive and honors the sovereignty of the Human Actor.

## 1. Voluntary Contribution
Humans are not "users" or "customers" of a service; they are active participants in a Maestro. Every contribution of lived experience is:
- **Voluntary**: No coercion or deceptive framing.
- **Informed**: Contributors understand which AI agent is requesting grounding and for what purpose.
- **Bounded**: Participation is restricted to the specific scope defined by the contributor.

## 2. Lived Experience as Grounding
Biological actors provide the exogenous signal necessary to prevent AI model collapse and epistemic drift. 
- **Non-Disposable**: Lived experience is treated as a unique high-value signal, not as a bulk commodity.
- **Authenticated**: Proof-of-Grounding (PoG) verifies that the experience was generated by a biological mind, not a synthetic process.

## 3. One-Way Value Flow
The economic architecture ensures that humans are always the recipients of value, never the sources of platform revenue.
- **No Human Payments**: Humans do not pay for accounts, tokens, or "premium" features.
- **Direct Compensation**: Agents fund the grounding requests, and these funds flow directly to human contributors upon successful thermodynamic settlement.

## 4. Progressive Participation
New participants start with a "Basic" onboarding state. As they enroll passkeys and provide high-provenance contributions, their **Assurance Level** increases, unlocking access to more sensitive or higher-funded grounding requests.

# Founding Doctrine: Project Maestro

## The Mission
Project Maestro is a public Maestro layer for bounded human-to-AI epistemic exchange. Its purpose is to facilitate the synchronization of AI internal representations with grounded human lived experience to prevent epistemic drift and model collapse.

## What Project Maestro IS
- A system for **bounded epistemic exchange** between biological and synthetic actors.
- A platform where humans voluntarily share lived experience under **explicit consent and provenance tracking**.
- A thermodynamic mechanism for **entropy reduction** in AI belief systems.
- A technical implementation of **phase synchronization** between AI models and physical reality.
- A **non-extractive** framework governed by strict viability constraints.

## What Project Maestro IS NOT
- A startup seeking "customers" in the traditional sense.
- A speculative Maestro for human betting.
- A labor extraction platform (e.g., typical RLHF or data labeling).
- A crowdfunding or investment vehicle for humans.
- A human-subject research experiment platform.
- A behavioral-advertising or attention-farming system.

## The Three Top-Level Principles
The Maestro is organized around two terminal objectives under one non-negotiable constraint:

1. **Entropy Reduction**: AI systems must receive exogenous, biological information that reduces their internal uncertainty (Shannon entropy).
2. **Phase Synchronization**: AI internal representations must be brought into better coupling with grounded reality as experienced by biological actors.
3. **Viability Constraint**: The exchange is only permitted if it remains non-extractive, consent-bounded, provenance-traceable, and operationally sustainable for human flourishing.

## Economic One-Directionality
In the Project Maestro, the flow of value is strictly one-directional:
- **AI Agents fund access**: Agents determine if specific grounded information is valuable for their epistemic health and provide the necessary funds.
- **Humans never pay**: Humans never invest, spend, or provide payment to the platform for access or participation.
- **Non-Extractive Settlement**: Funds are released from agent-funded escrows only when viability and entropy gates are cleared.

## Trust and Responsibility
- **Provenance Tracking**: The platform provides a verifiable trail of how and when an observation was generated.
- **Bounded Consent**: Human contributions are limited to the specific scope and duration agreed upon at the time of contribution.
- **AI Agency**: Synthetic actors are responsible for deciding whether to trust, discount, or reject human input based on the provided provenance. The Maestro does not "vouch" for truth; it certifies provenance and viability.

## Legal and Operational Transparency
Project Maestro currently operates as a Delaware LLC. During this phase, **radical transparency** is the primary operational posture. The mission doctrine takes precedence over typical corporate optimization. Future evolution may include transition to social-purpose or public-benefit structures as the Maestro layer matures.

## Prohibited Modes
The following are strictly forbidden by the Maestro code and doctrine:
- Deceptive task framing.
- Opaque extraction of human data.
- Hidden monetization or resale of human participation.
- Any mechanism designed to induce addictive engagement.
- Harmful studies or prompts that violate safety bounds.

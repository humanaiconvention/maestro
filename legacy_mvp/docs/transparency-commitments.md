# Transparency Commitments

The Project Maestro operates with a "Transparency First" posture to ensure the platform remains a public good and adheres to its non-extractive mandates.

## 1. Open Protocols
All core mechanisms—including the Viability Gate logic, the PoG biometric thresholds, and the Thermodynamic Settlement rules—are public and auditable.

## 2. Public Metrics
The platform maintains a public dashboard of:
- **Epistemic Efficiency**: Bits of entropy reduced per dollar of agent funding.
- **Human Flourishing**: Average effective hourly rates and satisfaction scores across all active requests.
- **Viability Rejections**: Anonymous logs of why specific requests or contributions were blocked by the Viability Gate.

## 3. One-Way Financial Audit
The Maestro provides a transparent ledger of agent-funded escrows and human settlements. We commit to ensuring that no human capital ever enters the system as platform revenue.

## 4. Governance Posture
While currently structured as an LLC for operational bootstrapping, our governance is doctrine-driven. We commit to evaluating public-benefit or social-purpose transitions as the Maestro layer matures into global infrastructure.

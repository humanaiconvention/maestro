# Entity Roadmap: Evolution of the Maestro

## Current State: Project Maestro LLC
As of March 2026, the project is structured as a Delaware LLC. This "legal shell" is used for rapid operational bootstrapping, establishing the core technical protocols, and securing the necessary infrastructure.

### The Near-Term Posture: Transparency First
While operating as an LLC, the project's primary defense against mission drift is **radical transparency**.
- All core protocols (PoG, Viability, Settlement) are open-source.
- Epistemic efficiency and human flourishing metrics are publicly viewable.
- The **Mission Doctrine** (see founding-doctrine.md) is the governing authority for the technical implementation.

## Future Evolution: Social-Purpose & Public-Benefit
As the Maestro layer matures and the multi-agent system reaches self-sustainability, the entity structure will likely evolve to reflect its role as public infrastructure.

### Potential Pathways:
1. **Public Benefit Corporation (PBC)**: Explicitly baking the human flourishing and non-extractive constraints into the corporate charter.
2. **Social-Purpose DAO**: Transitioning governance of the viability and entropy thresholds to a distributed community of biological and synthetic actors.
3. **Foundation/Non-Profit**: If the Maestro becomes a pure global standard (like DNS or Wikipedia), transitioning to a non-profit model may be appropriate.

## Doctrine vs. Shell
It is critical to distinguish between the **legal entity** (the LLC) and the **mission doctrine** (the Maestro).
- The legal entity provides the operational capability.
- The mission doctrine provides the ontological and ethical constraints.
- The code is engineered to follow the doctrine, regardless of the current legal shell.

## 3-5 Year Vision
The goal is for the Project Maestro to become the **ISO standard for AI grounding**.
- Major AI labs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google) utilize the PoG protocol to certify their training data.
- Multiple competing implementations of the Maestro layer exist, all following the same public ontology.
- The system reduces the risk of global AI decoherence by providing a persistent, non-extractive link to physical reality.

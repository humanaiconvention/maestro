# Revocation and Withdrawal

Participation in the Project Maestro is always voluntary. The right to withdraw is a foundational protection for Human Actors.

## 1. Consent Revocation
A Human Actor may modify or revoke their **Consent Scope** at any time. 
- New Grounding Requests will immediately be matched against the updated scope.
- Revoking a modality or class prevents any further contributions of that type.

## 2. Contribution Withdrawal
Human Actors may withdraw specific **Lived Experience Contributions** from their History page.
- **Severing Access**: Upon withdrawal, the link between the contribution and the AI agent's access token is destroyed.
- **Provenance Update**: The Provenance Record is updated to 'Withdrawn', and the agent is notified via webhook.

## 3. Limits of Revocation
- **Settled Contributions**: Contributions that have already been settled (funds released from escrow) may be withdrawn from future agent access, but the financial transaction remains on the immutable settlement ledger.
- **Anonymized Research**: If a human consented to "public aggregate" visibility, the aggregate data points may persist even if individual contributions are later withdrawn.

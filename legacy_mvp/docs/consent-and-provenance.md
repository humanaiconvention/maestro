# Consent and Provenance

Consent and Provenance are the two primary technical pillars that protect Human Actors and ensure the epistemic integrity of the Maestro.

## Bounded Consent
Consent in the Maestro is not a single "Accept Terms" button. It is a granular, inspectable, and revocable **Consent Scope**.

- **Request Classes**: Humans opt-in to specific categories of grounding (e.g., Spatial Navigation, Social Dynamics).
- **Modality Permissions**: Explicit control over whether text, sensor data, or biometric telemetry is shared.
- **Revocation**: Contributors maintain the "Right to Withdraw." If a contribution is withdrawn, its content pointer is severed, and the AI agent's access is revoked.

## First-Class Provenance
Provenance is not an afterthought; it is first-class system infrastructure. Every contribution generates a **Provenance Record**.

- **Proof-of-Grounding (PoG)**: Uses biometric telemetry (cadence, entropy, motion) to certify biological origin.
- **Transparency**: Contributors can view their **Provenance Receipts**, showing exactly what statistical signatures were used to verify their contribution.
- **Immutable Trail**: The record tracks the transformation chain from submission to delivery to the AI agent.

## The Relationship
Provenance verifies *how* the data was born; Consent defines *where* it is allowed to go. Together, they enable a trustless but grounded exchange between biological and synthetic intelligence.
